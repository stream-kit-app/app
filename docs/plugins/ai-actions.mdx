# AI Actions

The AI Actions plugin turns a description such as "when someone types !hug in chat, play hug.mp3 and reply in chat" into a draft [action](/docs/guide/actions). The draft opens in the regular action editor, so you can check and adjust the triggers, conditions and handlers before you save.

## Requirements

- A signed-in Stream Kit account with an active subscription
- The plugins whose triggers and handlers you want to use (for example [Twitch](/docs/plugins/twitch) for chat, [OBS](/docs/plugins/obs) for scenes)

## Pages

Open **AI Actions** in the sidebar:

| Page | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Create with AI** | Describe an action, generate a draft, and manage unpublished drafts |

## How it works

1. The plugin collects the triggers and handlers of your **enabled** plugins, including field options such as OBS scenes.
2. Your description and that list are sent to the Stream Kit cloud, which asks Claude (Anthropic) to build the action.
3. The result is checked against your installed triggers and handlers. When something does not match, the AI gets one chance to correct it.
4. The draft is stored under **Drafts** and opens in the action editor.
5. When you save it in the editor, the action is published to your actions and removed from **Drafts**.

## Drafts

Every generated action is kept as a draft until you publish it, so you can close the editor and come back later. From the **Drafts** list you can open a draft in the editor again or delete it. Drafts sync with your Stream Kit account like other plugin data.

Anything the AI could not build (for example a handler from a plugin you have not enabled) is listed under **Check the draft**.

## Example prompts

- When someone types !hug in chat, play hug.mp3 and reply with a hug message
- When someone follows: if their name is bob, say hi bob, otherwise welcome them by name
- When a raid has more than 10 viewers, switch to the OBS scene Raid

## Limits

- Generations are limited per account per day; the page shows how many are left.
- Descriptions can be up to 2000 characters.
- Branching (If handlers) can be nested up to three levels deep.
- Always review the draft before saving: the AI can pick the wrong trigger or option.
